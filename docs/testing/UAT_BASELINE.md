# UAT baseline

The approved v0.7 design is the acceptance baseline. UAT must validate role-specific journeys rather than only page rendering.

- Grants Office: opportunity to submission and funder outcome.
- Researcher: assignment response, proposal preparation, tasks and reports.
- Director: portfolio oversight and governed approvals.
- Finance: budget clearance, receipts, expenditure, commitments, reconciliation and financial reporting.
- Procurement: plan, requisition, supplier, purchase order, contract and asset tracking.
- Laboratory: grant-related procurement, consumables, equipment, maintenance/calibration and compliance.
- Governance: ethics/regulatory references, declarations and compliance deadlines.
- Post-award: contracts, partners, due diligence, amendments, deliverables, risks and closeout.
- Administration: users/roles, delegation, rules, workflow, forms, templates, records, imports/exports and audit.
- IT: system assurance, integrations, support, security events, backups/recovery and access reviews.
- Superadmin: protected technical controls without implicit business approval authority.

All lists requiring operational use must provide search and/or status/date filtering. All material writes must be auditable.
