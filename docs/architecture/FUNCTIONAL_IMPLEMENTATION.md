# Functional implementation map

The production application follows the approved NHRC Grants v0.7 institutional operations design.

## Governed lifecycle
Discover -> Eligibility review -> Director/team decision -> Researcher assignment -> Researcher response -> Preparation -> Internal review -> Institutional approval -> Submission -> Funder outcome -> Award conversion.

## Functional domains
- Executive and portfolio intelligence
- Pre-Award and proposal development
- Award Management, contracts, partners, due diligence, amendments, deliverables and risks
- Finance & Accounts, receipts, expenditure, commitments, partner advances, reconciliation and closeout
- Procurement, suppliers, requisitions, purchase orders and assets
- Laboratory grant oversight, consumables, equipment, maintenance/calibration and compliance
- Research Governance, declarations, ethics/regulatory links, privacy and legal review
- Performance, reports, outputs, impact and closeout
- People & Organisation, researcher, partner and funder master data
- Personal work, notifications and calendar
- Administration, workflow/rule configuration, forms, templates, imports/exports, records, access reviews and audit
- IT & Security, integrations, events, backups, recovery tests, continuity and privileged operations

## Control principles
Business approval authority is separate from technical administration. All material transitions are server governed. Financial concepts distinguish approved budget, cash received, expenditure and commitments. Audit records are append-oriented. Secrets remain outside source control.
